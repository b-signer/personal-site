About Me

I'm Benjamin, born and raised in Los Angeles and now pursuing a BS in Computer Science with minors in Mathematics and the Philosophy of Cognitive Science at UC San Diego. I've always been drawn to puzzles: the kind that take weeks to crack and teach you something in the process. That instinct led me to software engineering, and this past summer it took me to Genentech, where I built lab-automation tools for pharmaceutical researchers.

When I'm not writing code, I'm hiking, hunting down new indie music, keeping a detailed log of every film I watch, or playing golf — I captained my high school's varsity team. I also spent a quarter deep in engineering ethics research, which shaped how I think about the work I build and who ends up affected by it. Turns out those questions don't go away once you leave the classroom.

Projects

Thinking, Fast and Slow — Dual-Model Reasoning Agent

A multi-agent reasoning system inspired by Kahneman's Thinking, Fast and Slow. It routes each query between a fast, lightweight "System 1" model and a slower, high-reasoning "System 2" model, with a deterministic memory cache and confidence-based escalation. On my evaluation set it cut cost 6.3× while holding 98.5% of the accuracy.

Python OpenRouter Multi-Agent Evaluation
