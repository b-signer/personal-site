About Me

I'm Benjamin, born and raised in Los Angeles and now pursuing a BS in Computer Science with a minor in Mathematics at UC San Diego. I've always been drawn to puzzles: the kind that take weeks to crack and teach you something in the process. That instinct led me to software engineering, and this summer it's taking me to Genentech, where I'll be building lab automation tools for pharmaceutical researchers.

When I'm not writing code, I'm hiking, hunting down new indie music, keeping a detailed log of every film I watch, or playing golf — I captained my high school's varsity team. I also spent a quarter deep in engineering ethics research, which shaped how I think about the work I build and who ends up affected by it. Turns out those questions don't go away once you leave the classroom.
